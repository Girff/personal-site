ABOUT ME the part where I talk about myself

Hi — I'm Advay. I'm a programmer who got into code through games and never really found a reason to leave.

So far that's looked like: building a platformer about a penguin in a cave, helping run my school's Computer Science Club as an officer, and captaining our UIL Computer Science team to the Texas state competition — where we placed 4th.

I like problems that look impossible for the first twenty minutes, things that feel good to click on (see: this entire website), and shipping stuff instead of just talking about it.
