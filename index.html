ABOUT ME the part where I talk about myself

Hi, I’m Advay 👋. I’m someone who lives by one main philosophy… if something isn’t difficult or interesting to do, it isn’t worth doing. Everything I do is in search of another problem to solve. This obsession with problem-solving has taken form in my love for programming.

I’m an avid videogame player, and have used computers all my life — right down to building two desktops from parts and being the person everyone calls when a device, a driver, or the wifi stops cooperating. That same itch turned into passion projects including entire videogames, through which I learned full-stack agile development. Additionally, I value teaching others, and I am running my school’s Computer Science Club as an elected officer — planning meetings and giving instructional presentations to 50+ active members. I even captained my school’s Computer Science UIL team to reach the state-level competition – for the first time in my school’s 20-year history – winning fourth place. In the classroom that adds up to a 4.0 unweighted GPA and a 5 on the AP Computer Science A exam.

If I’m not programming or playing videogames, there are many things I may be doing instead: practicing my typing speed, watching movies (see the movie corner for more) or playing piano. I’ve been playing piano for over ten years, it’s something I truly couldn’t live without. Both of those hobbies have followed me out into the world — I volunteer with the Plano Symphony Orchestra helping concerts start on time, and with the Indica Film Festival running check-in and screening logistics.

This site is my deck. Look through it, drag things around, see what I’m holding.
